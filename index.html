Your Stakflow from seed to harvest

Five stages take most herbs from a packet of seeds to your plate.

🌰
Step 1

Sow

Press seeds into moist seed mix in a pot with drainage holes.

🌱
Step 2

Sprout

Keep the soil lightly moist and warm until seedlings appear.

☀️
Step 3

Grow

Move to a bright spot with several hours of sun and thin crowded seedlings.

✂️
Step 4

Pinch

Snip the top of young stems so the plant grows bushy, not tall.

🍃
Step 5

Harvest

Pick a little often, and never take more than a third at once.

Five friendly herbs to begin with 🌿

Choose two or three that you already cook with, so every leaf gets used.

Basil

Loves warmth and plenty of sun. Pinch flower buds.

☀️ 6+ hours of sun

Mint

Spreads fast, so keep it in its own pot.

🌤️ Part sun, moist soil

Coriander

Prefers cooler weather and can flower quickly in heat.

🌤️ Morning sun

Parsley

Slow to sprout, then steady. Cut outer stems first.

☀️ 4 to 6 hours

Chives

Hardy and easy. Snip near the base for fresh growth.

☀️ 6 hours of sun

💧 Watering tips

  • Check the soil with a fingertip before watering.
  • Water in the morning so leaves dry during the day.
  • Water until it drains, then empty the saucer.
  • Small pots dry out faster, so check them more often.

Quick fixes for common problems 🛠️

Tall, thin, floppy stems
Not enough light. Move to a brighter window and pinch the tips.
Yellowing leaves
Often too much water. Let the soil dry and check drainage.
Herb flowers too soon
Heat or stress. Pinch flower buds and harvest more often.
Tiny insects on leaves
Rinse leaves with water and isolate the pot until clear.

Do I need a garden?

No. A sunny windowsill, balcony or shelf near a bright window is enough for most herbs.

Most like four to six hours or more of light a day. Check each herb's card for details.
